PhotoRecull can **move and permanently delete** files on your computer. Its results depend
on what you select in the Google Photos Picker: any photo you forget to select is treated

You're responsible for reviewing the candidates before acting on them, and for any files you
move or delete. Use the default dry run and the stage mode first, and keep backups of
anything you can't afford to lose.
